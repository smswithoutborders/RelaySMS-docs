RelaySMS currently supports 5 platforms:

- Gmail
- Bluesky
- Telegram
- RelaySMS Mail (your personal RelaySMS email address)
- Mastodon

#### Go to the Platforms tab and tap the platform you want to save.

When a platform is saved it will light up. Tap it to see which account is connected.

<img src="/mockup/no-platform.png" alt="Saved Accounts Screen" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/mockup/accounts-saved.png" alt="Saved Accounts Screen" class="resized-image"/>


### Gmail

1. Tap Gmail.
2. Choose your Gmail account.
3. Grant permissions.
4. Tap Continue.

<img src="/mockup/gmail-accounts.png" alt="Gmail account" class="resized-image"/>

### Bluesky

1. Tap Bluesky.
2. Sign in to your Bluesky account.
3. Tap Authorize.

<img src="/mockup/bluesky-accounts.png" alt="Bluesky account" class="resized-image"/>

### Telegram

1. Tap Telegram.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Enter the code sent to your Telegram account.
4. Tap Submit.

<img src="/mockup/telegram-accounts.png" alt="Telegram account image" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/mockup/enter-telgram-number.png" alt="Enter Telegram number image" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/mockup/verify-telegram-code.png" alt="Verify Telegram code image" class="resized-image"/>


### RelaySMS Mail

1. Tap RelaySMS Mail.
2. Choose whether to receive your code via Signal or Telegram.
3. Tap Request Code.
4. Enter the code.
5. Done. Your number is authenticated.

<img src="/mockup/rmail-accounts.png" alt="RelaySMS mail account" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/mockup/platform-select.png" alt="Platform to receive code" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/mockup/rmail-phone-input.png" alt="Phone number input screen" class="resized-image"/>

> Tip: By default you always have your RelaySMS mail anonymous(random alias) account - you can use it to send messages quickly without verifying your phone number.

Anonymous vs authenticated:
You can use RelaySMS Mail without saving it (anonymous), but if you save it, you can receive replies by SMS during a shutdown.


### Mastodon

1. Tap Mastodon.
2. . Sign in to your Mastodon account.
3. Tap Authorize.
