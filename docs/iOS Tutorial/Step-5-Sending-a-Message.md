### Ways to send

Tap the compose button on the home screen. The **Send with...** menu opens. You can send in two ways.

<img src="/ios-mockup/compose-send-with.png" alt="Send with menu" class="resized-image"/>

#### Way 1 - Send quickly with R-Mail (no setup needed)

Best for: when you are caught in a shutdown and need to send an email quickly.

R-Mail is always available and needs no account. It sends from a random address, for example 123abc@relaysms.me. You cannot get replies to this address.

1. Tap the compose button on the home screen.
2. Tap the option at the bottom of the **Send with...** menu (**No account. Sends from a random address**).
3. Type the receiver's email address, a subject and your message.
4. Tap the send icon at the top.
5. Choose a routing number and send. See [Choose a routing number and send](#choose-a-routing-number-and-send).

<img src="/ios-mockup/rmail-compose.png" alt="R-Mail compose screen" class="resized-image"/>

#### Way 2 - Send with saved platforms

Best for: when you want to send from your own Gmail, Bluesky, Mastodon or Telegram account. You need to [save the platform](Step-3-Save-Platforms) first.

1. Tap the compose button on the home screen.
2. Choose your platform from the **Send with...** menu.
3. If you have more than one account saved, tap **Select an account** and choose one.
4. Type your message.
5. Tap the send icon at the top.
6. Choose a routing number and send.

### Choose a routing number and send

After you tap send, the **Routing Countries** screen opens.

1. Check the number under **Routing address**. This is the number your message will be sent to.
2. To use a different number, tap it under **Available routing numbers**, then tap **Set as default**.
3. Tap the send icon.

<img src="/ios-mockup/send-routing-number.png" alt="Routing countries screen" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/ios-mockup/routing-set-default.png" alt="Set as default routing number" class="resized-image"/>
<img src="/ios-mockup/routing-default-changed.png" alt="New default routing number" class="resized-image"/>

4. iOS opens a **New Message** screen with your encrypted message. Tap the send arrow.

<img src="/ios-mockup/send-sms.png" alt="New message screen with encrypted message" class="resized-image"/>

Do not panic if your SMS looks like random letters and numbers. That is expected encryption.

Your message now appears on the **Recent** tab.

<img src="/ios-mockup/home-message-sent.png" alt="Sent message on the Recent tab" class="resized-image"/>
