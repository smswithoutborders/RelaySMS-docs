> *Important: RelaySMS stores your saved platforms on your device. If you delete the app or switch phones, you can lose them. Back up regularly.*

Backups are end-to-end encrypted and saved to a folder on your device. To restore one, you need the backup file and its **recovery key**.

### Back up

1. Tap the **...** menu (top right corner of the home screen).
2. Tap **Backups**.
3. Read about on-device backups and tap **Continue**.
4. Tap **Choose Folder** and pick where your backup will be stored.
5. Tap **View Recovery Key**.
6. Copy your 64-character recovery key and store it somewhere safe, like a password manager. Don't share it with anyone.
7. Tap **Continue**.

> *If you lose your recovery key, you won't be able to restore your backup.*

### Manage your backup

1. Tap the **...** menu, then **Backups**.
2. From here you can:
   - Tap **Back Up Now** to update your backup.
   - See the date of your **Last Backup**.
   - Tap **View Recovery Key** to see your key again.
   - Tap **Delete Backup** to remove it.

<img src="/ios-mockup/backup-4.png" alt="On-device backups screen" class="resized-image"/>

### Restore

> *Important: You can only restore a backup on the welcome screen during onboarding. If you don't restore then, you won't be able to restore later.*

1. Install a new copy of RelaySMS and open it.
2. On the welcome screen, tap **Restore backup**.
3. Tap **Choose your backup file**.
4. Find and select your backup file. It is named **RelaySMS-Backup** followed by the date.
5. Enter your recovery key.
6. Tap **Next**. Your platforms are restored.
