Saving a platform lets you send messages from your own account. You need an internet connection to save a platform, so do it before a shutdown.

RelaySMS on iOS currently supports 4 platforms:

- Bluesky
- Gmail
- Mastodon
- Telegram

> You don't need to save anything to send an email with **R-Mail**. See [Sending a message](Step-5-Sending-a-Message).

### Save an account

1. Go to the **Platforms** tab and tap the platform you want to save.
2. Tap **Add new**.
3. If iOS asks to let RelaySMS sign in, tap **Continue**.
4. Sign in to your account and allow access. For **Telegram**, enter your phone number and the code sent to your Telegram app.
5. Your account appears in the list.

Repeat for each platform you want to use. The number next to each platform shows how many accounts you have saved for it.

<img src="/ios-mockup/platforms-saved.png" alt="Platforms with saved accounts" class="resized-image"/>

### Remove an account

1. Go to the **Platforms** tab and tap the platform.
2. Tap the red bin icon next to the account.

> *Remember to [back up](Step-4-Back-Up-and-Restore#back-up) after you save or remove a platform.*
