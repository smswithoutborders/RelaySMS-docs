When you first open RelaySMS, you'll see the welcome screen. You can change the app language with the button at the top right.

>*If you have a previous backup, tap **Restore backup** now. This is the only opportunity to restore a backup. See [Restore](Step-4-Back-Up-and-Restore#restore) for more information.*

<img src="/ios-mockup/welcome.png" alt="Welcome screen" class="resized-image"/>

Tap **Learn how it works** to go through a few short screens.

RelaySMS no longer asks you to log in or register. Your accounts are saved securely on your device so you can send messages as yourself. Tap **Done** on the last screen.

<img src="/ios-mockup/onboarding-save-accounts.png" alt="Save access to your accounts screen" class="resized-image"/>

When you finish, you'll land on the home screen. It has three tabs at the bottom: **Recent**, **Platforms** and **Routing numbers**.

<img src="/ios-mockup/home-empty.png" alt="Home screen" class="resized-image"/>
